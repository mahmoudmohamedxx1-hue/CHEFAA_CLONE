# Chefaa.com Medications Category Product Listing Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of the Medications product listing page on Chefaa.com, an Egyptian online pharmacy platform, examining layout, functionality, and user experience features.

**URL**: https://chefaa.com/eg-ar/now/category/medications  
**Analysis Date**: November 1, 2025

## Product Layout & Display Format

### **Grid Layout System**
- **Layout Type**: Grid view with organized product presentation
- **Products per Row**: 4 products per row in main content area
- **Card Design**: Clean, consistent product card format with uniform spacing

### **Product Information Display**
Each product card includes:
- **Product Image**: High-quality, clear product photography
- **Product Name**: Full Arabic product title with key specifications (e.g., "كونترولوك 20 ملجم | 14 قرص | 2 شريط")
- **Price**: Clearly displayed in Egyptian Pounds (جنيه) - examples: "100 جنيه", "54 جنيه"
- **Add to Cart Button**: Prominent "اضف الى العربة" (Add to Cart) button
- **Stock Indicators**: "كمية محدودة" (Limited Quantity) labels where applicable
- **Interactive Elements**: All components (image, name, price) are clickable links to product detail pages

## Pagination System

### **Pagination Features**
- **Extensive Catalog**: 134+ pages of products indicating large inventory
- **Navigation Controls**: 
  - Previous « and ‹ buttons
  - Numbered page navigation (1, 2, 3, etc.)
  - Next › and » buttons
- **URL Structure**: Clean pagination with parameters (`?page=2`)
- **User Experience**: Standard pagination familiar to online shoppers

## Sorting Options

### **Available Sorting Methods**
Three main sorting options via dropdown:
1. **المتاح فقط** (Available only) - Filter to show in-stock items only
2. **السعر : من الأعلى إلى الأقل** (Price: High to Low) - Descending price order
3. **السعر : من الأقل إلى الأعلى** (Price: Low to High) - Ascending price order

## Filtering System

### **Comprehensive Filter Categories**

#### **Structural Filters**
- **الأقسام الأساسية** (Main Sections) - Primary product categories
- **الأقسام الفرعية** (Sub Sections) - Secondary categories
- **الأقسام الفرعية الثانوية** (Secondary Sub Sections) - Tertiary categories

#### **Product Attribute Filters**
- **الماركات** (Brands) - Manufacturer/brand selection
- **النوع** (Type) - Product type classification
- **الحجم** (Size) - Package/volume variations
- **التركيز** (Concentration) - Medication strength levels
- **اللون** (Color) - Product color options
- **الفئة العمرية** (Age Group) - Age-appropriate selections

#### **Specialized Filters**
- **نوع البشرة** (Skin Type) - For skincare products
- **نوع الشعر** (Hair Type) - For haircare products
- **العناية بالأسنان** (Dental Care) - Oral health products
- **مناسب ل** (Suitable for) - Target audience specifications
- **خالي من** (Free from) - Allergen-free options
- **مميزات خاصة** (Special Features) - Unique product characteristics
- **الرائحة** (Scent) - Fragrance variations
- **المذاق** (Taste) - Flavor options

#### **Price Filter**
- **السعر** (Price) - Range slider: 0 to 8,000 Egyptian Pounds
- **مسح الكل** (Clear All) - One-click filter reset
