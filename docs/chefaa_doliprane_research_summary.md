### Indications & Usage
**Primary Uses:**
- Headaches and migraines
- Toothache and earache
- Menstrual pain
- Muscle, joint, and bone pain
- Fever reduction

**Dosage Instructions:**
- One tablet 2-4 times daily
- Minimum 4-hour intervals between doses
- Maximum 4 tablets per day
- Can be taken on empty stomach or after food

### Safety Information
**Contraindications:**
- Allergy to Paracetamol
- Liver or kidney diseases
- Concurrent use with epilepsy/TB medications
- Anticoagulants (Warfarin) use
- St. John's Wort interaction
- Other Paracetamol-containing medicines

**Special Considerations:**
- Safe for pregnant and breastfeeding women (lowest effective dose)
- Recommended for people with high blood pressure
- Does not typically cause drowsiness
- Overdosing can cause severe liver problems
