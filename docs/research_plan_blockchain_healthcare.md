# Research Plan: Blockchain Applications in Healthcare and Pharmaceutical Supply Chain (2024-2025)

## Task Overview
Research blockchain applications in healthcare and pharmaceutical supply chain for 2024-2025, focusing on six key areas:
1. Drug provenance tracking and anti-counterfeiting
2. Smart contracts for prescription fulfillment  
3. Patient data privacy and consent management
4. Clinical trial data integrity
5. Supply chain transparency
6. Pharmaceutical tokenization and incentives

## Research Strategy
- Task Type: Search-Focused (comprehensive information gathering)
- Time Period: Focus on 2024-2025 developments and current state
- Sources: Academic papers, industry reports, company announcements, government initiatives
- Verification: Cross-reference findings from multiple authoritative sources
