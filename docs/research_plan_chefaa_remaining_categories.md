## Objective
Extract comprehensive product information from 5 categories on Chefaa.com: Makeup, Medical Supplies, Vitamins, Sexual Health, and Pet Supplies.

## Task Complexity Assessment
**Complex Task** - Requires systematic website navigation, data extraction from multiple categories, and structured data compilation.

3. Vitamins
4. Sexual Health

- Prices

- Ratings
- Stock status
- Specifications
