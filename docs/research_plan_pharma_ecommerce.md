# Pharmaceutical E-Commerce Platforms Competitive Analysis Research Plan

## Research Objective
Analyze the most advanced pharmaceutical e-commerce platforms in 2024-2025, focusing on Amazon Pharmacy, CVS Health, Walgreens Boots, and emerging players. Identify unique value propositions, technology innovations, user experience breakthroughs, business model innovations, and partnership strategies to find gaps and opportunities for differentiation.

**Type**: Verification-Focused Task with Deep Analysis
**Complexity**: High - requires comprehensive market analysis, competitor evaluation, and strategic insights
