## Key Insights from Visual Analysis

### Product Card Structure Observed:
Each product card consistently contains:
- Product image (high quality, standardized sizing)
- Product name in Arabic (primary text)
- Brand name (prominently displayed)
- Price in Egyptian Pounds (EGP)
- "Add to Cart" button (indicates stock status)
- Clickable product title and price links

### Navigation Elements:
- Breadcrumb navigation (Home > Category > Subcategory)
- Main category navigation bar
- Sidebar with subcategories
- Pagination controls
- Search functionality
- Location/delivery settings

### Category Structure:
Daily Essentials category includes:
- Body & Bath Care (العناية بالجسم و الاستحمام)
- Oral & Dental Care (العناية بالفم و الاسنان)
- Feminine Care (العناية النسائية) 
- Men's Care (العناية الرجالية)
- Protection (الحماية)
- Natural Herbs & Vitamins (الأعشاب الطبيعية و الفيتامينات)

## Research Limitations Encountered

1. **Page 51 Accessibility:** Despite multiple attempts, page 51 of Daily Essentials could not be reliably accessed
2. **Navigation Consistency:** Website showed inconsistent navigation behavior
3. **URL Persistence:** URLs would change during page interactions
4. **Dynamic Content:** Some content appeared to load dynamically affecting analysis

## Alternative Access Strategies Needed

Given the navigation challenges, successful product extraction from page 51 would require:
1. Manual verification of page existence
2. Alternative navigation paths through subcategories
3. Direct product URL access
4. Website interface exploration rather than direct URL access
